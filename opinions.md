# My Personality, Interests and Opinions

## My Learning Personality

I prefer learning through practical examples rather than only reading theoretical explanations. I like following instructions step by step and testing things myself.

## My Interests

I am interested in:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Software Engineering
- Computer Science
- Machine Learning
- Mobile Application Development
- Cybersecurity
- Database Management

## My Opinion About Artificial Intelligence

I believe artificial intelligence is an important technology that can make learning, problem-solving, and software development easier.

However, AI should be used as a learning assistant. Students should understand the concepts instead of blindly copying generated answers.

## My Opinion About Programming

I believe programming becomes easier with regular practice. Making mistakes is a normal part of learning, and debugging helps improve problem-solving skills.

## My Preferred Learning Style

I prefer:
- Simple and easy explanations.
- Step-by-step instructions.
- Practical coding examples.
- Clear solutions to errors.
- Testing each step before moving forward.

## My Goals

I want to become better at software development, explore modern AI technologies, and build useful applications.

## My Personal Approach

I believe consistency, patience, curiosity, and practical experience are important for becoming a good computer science professional.
