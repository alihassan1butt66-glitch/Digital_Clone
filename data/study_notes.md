## Large Language Models and RAG

Large Language Models can understand and generate human language. I am interested in learning how these models can be connected to external information.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, also known as RAG, is a method that retrieves relevant information before generating an answer.

A typical RAG system has several stages. First, documents are divided into smaller sections called chunks. Next, the chunks are indexed so they can be searched. When a user asks a question, the system retrieves relevant passages and sends them to a language model.

Hybrid retrieval combines keyword-based search with semantic search. Keyword search can find exact terms, while semantic search can find passages with similar meanings even when the wording is different.
